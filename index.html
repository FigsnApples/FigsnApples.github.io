01 / About

From a measurable signal
to a meaningful insight.

I’m a bioengineering PhD student at UC San Diego, working in the Joseph Wang Laboratory for Nanobioelectronics. My research brings together electrochemical sensing, wearable hardware, and physiological measurements.

I’m especially interested in what happens beyond the laboratory: how a sensor performs on people, what its measurements tell us, and how continuous monitoring can support clinical research and human performance.

My experience spans microneedle fabrication, PCB design, potentiostat development, embedded programming, signal processing, and human studies—with industry experience at Dexcom, Cepheid, and OptiScan Biomedical.
